1. The Secret Santa gift

You get one name and buy one gift for that person, up to R150. Keep who you drew a secret until the gifts are opened. Couples can’t draw each other, and nobody draws themselves.

2. The funny swap

Everyone also brings one wrapped gift worth up to R50. It should be funny and odd, and it is not for anyone in particular. Think silly, strange or useless. All the gifts go into one pile and are swapped on the day, so wrap it well and don’t put your name on it.
