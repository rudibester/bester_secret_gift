Bester Family Gift Exchange

Two gift swaps this year: a Secret Santa gift for one person, and a funny gift for the pile.

How it works

  1. Find your person. Choose your name below and type the 6-digit PIN you were sent. Only you can open your result.
  2. See who you’re buying for. You’ll see one family member’s name and their wish-list.
  3. Buy one gift, up to R150. The wish-list is a guide. Pick one thing or mix a few, and stay within R150.
  4. Keep it secret. Don’t tell anyone who you drew until the gifts are opened.
  5. Bring funny gift(s) too. One or more wrapped gifts, up to R50 in total, funny and odd, and not for anyone in particular. They go into the swap pile.

    The Secret Santa gift

    You get one name and buy one gift for that person, up to R150. Couples can’t draw each other, and nobody draws themselves. Gifts are opened together, and that’s when the secret comes out.

    The funny swap

    Everyone also brings one or more wrapped gifts that add up to R50 in total. They should be funny and odd, and they are not for anyone in particular. Think silly, strange or useless. All the gifts go into one pile and are swapped on the day, so wrap them well and don’t put your name on them.

    Cheap, wacky ideas:

    • Novelty socks or a silly hat
    • Fake moustaches, wigs or party props
    • A rubber chicken or a squeaky toy
    • A weird kitchen gadget (egg shaper, banana slicer)
    • Googly eyes to stick on everything
    • Fidget toys or a stress ball
    • Odd stationery (shaped erasers, a giant pencil)
    • A joke book or a gag mug
    • Bubbles, glow sticks or a kazoo
    • A mystery find from a second-hand shop (retro knick-knack, ugly ornament)
